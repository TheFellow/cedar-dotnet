   8 | 
   9 | UTC = local_time - offset
  10 | 
  11 | So `1970-01-01T00:00:00+0100` should yield `1969-12-31T23:00:00Z` (subtract 1 hour), not `1970-01-01T01:00:00Z`.
  12 | 
  13 | The Go fix is simply `t.Add(-offset)` instead of `t.Add(offset)` in `types/datetime.go` line ~221.
  14 | 
  15 | This is a correctness bug that will affect any C# code that manually parses datetime strings with timezone offsets and converts them to UTC milliseconds (Unix epoch).
